New wireless sale

Sales and wireless lines count by the date they were sold. Installs completed and cancellations count by install date, so they usually belong to earlier sales.

Pay report names

Some agents appear on the Verizon report under a different name. Map each report name to the right person so their pay report and payouts land correctly.

Device management

Verizon iPads assigned to each agent. Add the device ID (serial or asset tag) and an optional note, like the model or when it was issued. Agents can see their own device only.

Import pay report

Upload the weekly Excel report. Internet orders match by MON, wireless lines by phone number. Paid orders move to installed automatically, since Verizon pays after the install. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.

Payouts

Installed orders waiting to be paid, by agent. Check them off and tap Mark paid. Agents get an alert when you do.

Weekly goals

Weekly targets, Monday to Sunday. Progress shows on the Leaders tab, and everyone gets an alert when an agent hits their goal. Set a team default, then override it for anyone who needs a different number.

Per agent

Export orders

Download the board as a spreadsheet (opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). Good for payouts and keeping a backup.

Add a teammate

They sign in with this email and password. Nothing gets sent automatically; you'll get a message to text them.

Team

Deactivating someone blocks sign-in but keeps all their orders credited to them.

Put a new teammate forward

Send their details to an owner. Once it's approved they get a sign-in and join your team automatically. Right after you add them you'll get a welcome text to send with the onboarding link.

My team

The people reporting to you. You see their orders in full, get their install reminder texts, and can open their pay reports.

Waiting for approval

Names team leaders have put forward. Approving creates their sign-in and puts them on the leader's team. Nothing is sent automatically — you'll get a message to text them.

Applications

Onboarding forms sent in from /apply. Approving creates their sign-in and joins them to whoever referred them. ID photos and headshots delete themselves 90 days after approval; Social Security Numbers stay encrypted and are only shown when you tap to see one.
